## What You Will Build

Participants leave with a prioritized AI opportunity backlog, a lightweight evaluation framework for AI features, and a delivery plan they can bring back to their team.

## Who It Is For

This program is designed for product managers and innovation leads who need to scope, prioritize, and ship AI-assisted features without relying on engineering to translate every decision.

## Learning Approach

Sessions combine short instruction with structured working time on each learner's real product context, so the roadmap and criteria produced in class are usable the same week.
