## What This Covers

A shortcut track for leaders — starting from AI & GenAI Foundations rather than the full engineering path:

- AI and GenAI for leaders, and identifying AI opportunities
- AI strategy, responsible AI & ethics, and AI governance, risk & compliance
- AI economics & ROI, and build vs. buy vs. partner decisions
- AI vendor evaluation and AI operating models
- AI portfolio management, AI adoption & change management, and workforce transformation
- Enterprise AI transformation and an AI Leadership capstone project

Leadership learners can selectively enter Architect or Product courses later without completing the full engineering path.

## Who This Is For

Executives and leaders who need to set AI strategy and make sound AI investment decisions, without a hands-on engineering background.
