## What You Will Build

Participants create an AI adoption brief, a delivery operating model, and an evaluation checklist their teams can use after the program.

## Who It Is For

This program is designed for leaders who are responsible for introducing AI into engineering work without weakening quality, security, or team trust.

## Learning Approach

Sessions combine short instruction, structured discussion, and practical planning exercises. The emphasis is on decisions leaders can make with confidence.
