## What This Covers

Picks up where GenAI Builder ends and goes to production depth:

- Software engineering and data engineering for AI, enterprise APIs and integration
- Advanced RAG, context/state/memory engineering, knowledge graphs, and GraphRAG
- MCP and AI interoperability
- Advanced agentic AI and multi-agent systems
- Multimodal GenAI, model selection and benchmarking, fine-tuning and customization
- Production AI engineering: testing and reliability, security and red-teaming, observability, LLMOps, and cloud deployment and scaling
- An AI Engineer capstone project

A recurring principle throughout: build, evaluate, analyze failure, improve, regression test, deploy, monitor — evaluation-driven development as a habit, not a one-time exercise.

## Who This Is For

Software engineers and developers who've completed GenAI Builder and want a self-paced route to the same production AI Engineering depth as the live AI Engineer mentorship, on their own schedule.
