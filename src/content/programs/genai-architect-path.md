## What This Covers

A senior architecture path — recommended after Engineer or Forward Deployed Engineer competencies, not an easy direct continuation:

- AI system design, design patterns and anti-patterns, and GenAI architecture patterns
- Enterprise data architecture and enterprise integration architecture for AI
- Model selection architecture, enterprise RAG architecture, knowledge graph and GraphRAG architecture
- Agentic AI architecture, enterprise MCP/interoperability architecture, and multimodal architecture
- AI security architecture, responsible AI and governance architecture
- Reliability and resilience, observability architecture, cloud and scalability architecture, and AI cost architecture / FinOps
- Architecture trade-offs and ADRs, build-vs-buy and vendor evaluation
- Enterprise AI platform architecture and a GenAI Architect capstone project

Learners move from individual implementation techniques toward reusable architectural, deployment, and safety patterns.

## Who This Is For

Senior engineers and architects with AI Engineer or Forward Deployed Engineer experience who want the self-paced route to enterprise GenAI architecture depth.
