## What This Covers

The entry point to the self-paced library — a foundation track with no prior AI or ML knowledge assumed:

- **Working with modern AI assistants** — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, model selection, files, images, voice, research, and professional workflows
- **AI, ML & deep learning intuition** — features, labels, prediction, neural networks, training, and generalization, without heavy mathematics
- **Data, APIs, Python & SQL foundations** — structured and unstructured data, APIs, databases, and the coding basics AI work builds on
- **How LLMs and transformers work** — tokens, embeddings, attention, pretraining, context windows, and why models hallucinate
- **Responsible AI & ethics foundations** — fairness, bias, privacy, transparency, and accountability as a business discipline, not just a compliance checkbox

Every self-paced learner starts here before moving into GenAI Builder or a specific career path.

## Who This Is For

Anyone who wants the complete mental map before specializing — no prior AI or machine-learning background required.
