## What This Covers

The field lifecycle of translating a business problem into a production AI outcome:

- AI use-case discovery, AI product fundamentals, and requirements engineering for AI
- Business process mapping and enterprise data discovery
- Rapid AI prototyping and enterprise APIs & integration
- AI UX and conversational design
- Enterprise RAG & agent solutions built for real customer environments
- AI solution estimation, AI economics & ROI, and technical presales and solutioning
- Stakeholder communication, responsible AI for customer solutions, adoption and change management
- Production delivery and a Forward Deployed Engineer capstone project

The throughline: business problem → process → AI opportunity → data → POC → architecture → integration → evaluation → ROI → production → adoption.

## Who This Is For

Engineers, consultants, and technical professionals who've completed GenAI Builder and want the self-paced route to the same Forward Deployed Engineer skill set as the live mentorship.
