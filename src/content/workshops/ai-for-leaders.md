## Workshop Outcome

Leaders leave with a shared framework for evaluating AI opportunities, a first-pass operating model for AI-assisted engineering work, and a short list of governance decisions to make before scaling adoption.

## What We Cover

The session covers opportunity mapping for engineering workflows, delivery and review practices for AI-assisted code, and the governance questions leadership teams need to answer early.

## Best Fit

This workshop is best for engineering leadership teams who need alignment before rolling out AI tooling or practices across their organization.
