## Workshop Outcome

Participants leave with a small prompt playbook, review criteria, and examples of repeatable workflows that can be shared inside their team.

## What We Cover

The session focuses on prompt structure, reusable context, quality review, and the handoff from individual experimentation to team practice.

## Best Fit

This workshop is useful for teams that already use AI tools informally and want more consistent outcomes.
