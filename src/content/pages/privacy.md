## What this site collects

SkillUp's marketing website is statically generated — there is no server-side database, and no account or login data is collected here. (The separate learning platform, if you use it, has its own privacy terms.)

## Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use it — which pages are viewed, and which links and buttons are clicked. Analytics only runs on the live deployment; it does not run during local development or preview builds without a measurement ID configured.

## Contact enquiries

Contact links on this site open a pre-filled email to SkillUp's support address. Whatever you share in that email is used only to respond to your enquiry — it is not added to a mailing list or shared with third parties.

## Cookies

Google Analytics may set cookies in your browser to distinguish visitors. No other cookies are set by this site.

## Questions

For any privacy questions, contact SkillUp using the email address on the [Contact](../contact/) page.
